## Tatoeba / ManyThings bilingual sentences

The generated sentence bank is derived from the Mandarin Chinese / English sentence pairs distributed by [ManyThings](https://www.manythings.org/anki/) from the [Tatoeba Project](https://tatoeba.org/), snapshot `2026-02-13`.

The sentence pairs are distributed under [Creative Commons Attribution 2.0 France](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.0/fr/) (`CC BY 2.0 FR`). Each generated question retains the source sentence IDs and contributor attribution in `public/sentence-bank.json`, and the website displays that attribution with the answer explanation.
